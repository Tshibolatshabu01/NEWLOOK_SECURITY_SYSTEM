SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Security controls are part of the platform structure.

Public product messaging describes implemented architecture without claiming certifications or controls that have not been independently verified.

01

Tenant isolation

Company-owned data is structured below a company tenant boundary and access is evaluated against authenticated company context.

02

Authentication

Administrative identity is based on Firebase Authentication and the corresponding users/{uid} profile.

03

Role-based access

Supported administrative roles include super_admin, company_admin, operations_manager and supervisor.

04

Audit controls

Important platform and company actions can be recorded for operational traceability.

05

Operational monitoring

Realtime listeners and status indicators help management see relevant operational changes.

06

Firebase security

Firestore rules define tenant and role boundaries; live deployment testing remains part of production acceptance.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is YOURI a guard recruitment service?

No. YOURI is positioned as security management software for organisations that need to manage security operations.

Can multiple companies use the platform?

Yes. The architecture uses companyId as the central tenant boundary for company-owned operations.

Does YOURI include payroll?

Yes. Payroll is a separate operational module with controlled Security and Staff source contracts.

Can managers have different permissions?

Yes. Access is role-based and the platform supports company_admin, operations_manager and supervisor roles in addition to super_admin.

Does the platform require site-radius blocking?

No. Guard and Attendance operational actions are not designed to be blocked by site-radius verification. GPS may be captured as operational metadata.

Are public prices shown?

No fixed prices are displayed here because commercial plans and limits should be configured by the platform owner.
